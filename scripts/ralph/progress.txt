## Codebase Patterns
- Document reusable codebase patterns found during iterations.
- Centralize visual foundations in `src/app/styles/index.css` and treat `surface-base`, `surface-panel`, `surface-overlay`, `surface-shell`, `surface-interactive` as the only shared surface contracts.
- Keep shared feedback semantic by routing toast/dialog states through `feedback-*` classes and theme tokens instead of hardcoded red/green/blue Tailwind palettes.
- Avoid default glassmorphism in shared primitives; blur, glow and strong gradients should be opt-in accents for spotlight moments, not the baseline container treatment.
- Keep shell information architecture in a single shared navigation hook (`src/shared/components/navigation/use-shell-navigation.ts`) so mobile sheets, desktop sidebar and topbar context cannot drift apart.
- In the mobile shell, recurring flows stay on the fixed bottom bar while secondary/admin routes move to labeled sheets; safe-area padding belongs to the shell contract, not to individual pages.
